# Sources and provenance

The combined repository has two provenance layers:

1. **Component provenance** — each bundled study retains its original source, data, acknowledgment, and manifest files. CRPS's authoritative bibliography is `studies/cosmology/SOURCES.md`, with observational-data provenance under its `data/` directory.
2. **Integration-time verification** — `docs/source-checks.md` records the primary public sources used to recheck time-sensitive DESI and Bayesian-evidence claims on 2026-08-09.

For exact Recurrence Dynamics results, the source of truth is the bundled code, committed certificates/data, independent verifiers, and component manuscript. For CPS, the source of truth is its specification, timeline, and coordinate utility. For CRPS observational calculations, the source of truth is its declared configuration, bundled DESI projection/provenance, code, generated outputs, and cited external publications.
