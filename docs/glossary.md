# Glossary

**Complete state** — A state specification containing every degree of freedom needed by the declared dynamical law to determine future evolution.

**Exact recurrence** — Return of the complete state to exactly the same state after a nonzero time/step.

**Near recurrence** — Return within a declared metric and tolerance `epsilon`; not the same as exact recurrence.

**Observational recurrence** — Return of selected observables within a declared resolution, regardless of whether the complete state returned.

**Predictive equivalence** — Distinct complete states that generate the same future observation sequence under a declared observation map.

**Hidden-state ambiguity** — Failure of the current observation to distinguish complete states that can have different futures.

**Period `T`** — Least positive step count for exact return of a state in a discrete recurrent dynamics.

**Phase** — Location within a known recurrent orbit/cycle. Meaningful only after the dynamics/cycle are defined.

**Interaction fiber** — In Recurrence Dynamics, the subset of HPP states compatible with specified exact invariants after separating transport/geometry from nonlinear interaction dynamics.

**`M` macrostate** — CPS operational epoch label. `M0` radiation dominated, `M1` matter dominated before reference acceleration onset, `M2` transition after acceleration begins and before dark-energy equality, `M3` dark-energy-dominated acceleration, `M4` project-defined deep-dark-energy epoch.

**`Phi_C`** — CPS composition phase, `(2/pi) atan(rho_DE/rho_m)`, bounded between 0 and 1. It is not a recurrence phase and not a percentage of total cosmic lifetime.

**Stable eternal de Sitter branch** — A conditional future in which positive vacuum energy persists indefinitely and the spacetime approaches de Sitter behavior.

**Metastable de Sitter branch** — A de Sitter-like phase that may decay to another vacuum before any recurrence event.

**Thermodynamic de Sitter recurrence scale** — Conventional entropy-exponential scale `H_Lambda^-1 exp(S_dS/k_B)` under finite-patch assumptions. Not synonymous with a quantum epsilon-recurrence time.

**Quantum epsilon recurrence** — Return within a quantum-state distance tolerance `epsilon` under a specified finite-system Hamiltonian/spectrum theorem.

**Partial identification** — Statistical situation in which available data and assumptions determine only a set/interval of possible parameter values rather than one unique value. CRPS's unrestricted recurrence-probability result is `[0,1]`; because this is the full logical probability range, it is specifically a non-identifiability result rather than an informative probability estimate.
