# Scientific boundaries and nonclaims

This file is intentionally strict. It defines what the combined repository does **not** establish.

## Finite-model results do not prove cosmic finiteness

The exact recurrence and phase-navigation results in Recurrence Dynamics are statements about explicitly finite reversible models. They show what can be proved when the state space and update law are known. They do not show that the physical universe has a finite number of states, periodic boundary conditions, HPP collisions, or a finite recurrence period.

## A repeated observation is not necessarily a repeated complete state

A density field, image, particle-position snapshot, cosmological parameter vector, or other coarse observation can omit variables needed to determine the future. Claims of recurrence must declare the complete state or explicitly state that the claim is only observational or macroscopic recurrence.

## CPS is not a recurrence coordinate

`M3 / Phi_C=0.7376` is a coordinate on a flat matter+Lambda reference cosmology. `M` is a reference macrostate and `Phi_C` is a bounded dark-energy/matter composition phase. Neither quantity counts recurrence cycles or identifies a recurrence period.

## A Big Crunch does not imply exact recurrence

Contraction is not automatically time reversal. A bounce is not automatically a reset. Entropy production, particle creation, hidden degrees of freedom, vacuum transitions, and asymmetric bounce physics can prevent state return even if the scale factor repeatedly expands and contracts.

## Empty space does not define a unique restart state

“Everything disappears” is not a complete physical state specification. Vacuum structure, fields, geometry, horizons, quantum state, topology, and boundary conditions can remain. A claim that an empty state necessarily recreates the same Big Bang requires an explicit dynamical model that demonstrates it.

## de Sitter thermodynamic recurrence is conditional

The entropy-exponential de Sitter estimate assumes a stable eternal de Sitter-like branch and an effectively finite recurrent causal patch. It should not be reported as an observationally measured recurrence time or as a rigorous quantum epsilon-recurrence time for the universe.

## Quantum recurrence needs microscopic input

Finite-system quantum recurrence theorems require conditions such as a finite discrete spectrum plus a specified metric/tolerance. Cosmological observations do not presently identify the necessary Hamiltonian spectrum and energy span. The framework therefore leaves the cosmological epsilon-recurrence time uncalculated.

## Probability remains partially identified

Data can constrain parameters within selected cosmological models and can inform selected model-family comparisons. They do not uniquely determine the universe's ultimate branch or all recurrence-theory assumptions. Without those theory priors, the unrestricted recurrence probability remains `[0,1]`. This is a partial-identification/non-identifiability statement: the data do not narrow the target beyond its logical bounds, and the interval must not be interpreted as a probability estimate or uniform belief distribution.


## Observer-indistinguishability is observer-relative

A state pair being indistinguishable under one declared observation map does not make the complete states identical. Conversely, a tiny microscopic mismatch can become observable quickly if the dynamics amplify it. Any claim that two cosmological histories are “indistinguishable to us” must specify the observer model, accessible measurements, causal domain, noise/resolution, observation horizon, and tolerance. The repository does not invent a numerical human-observer threshold.

## Predictive equivalence is not automatically ontological identity

The protocol-family predictive quotient identifies descriptions that generate the same predictions under the declared admissible protocols. This proves predictive equivalence, not metaphysical identity. Identifying quotient classes with all of physical reality requires an additional interpretive principle.

## Observer ambiguity is not fundamental indistinguishability by default

The exact HPP density doubletons are permanently indistinguishable to the density observer, but richer HPP sensors reconstruct the full state. The ambiguity is therefore observer-map dependent in the benchmark. It must not be described as a fundamental inability of the underlying HPP theory to distinguish the states.
