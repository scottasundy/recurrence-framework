# Sources and scientific provenance

## Observational cosmology

1. DESI Collaboration / M. Abdul Karim et al. (2025), **DESI DR2 Results II: Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations and Cosmological Constraints**, *Physical Review D* 112, 083515, arXiv:2503.14738. This is the principal observational paper associated with the DR2 BAO cosmology chains used here.

2. DESI DR2 BAO cosmology products, official data documentation and Cobaya chains: `https://data.desi.lbl.gov/public/papers/y3/bao-cosmo-params/README.html`. The exact chain provenance and DESI-published hashes are recorded in `data/desi-source.md`.

3. DESI Collaboration (2026), **DESI DR2 Results IV: Alcock-Paczynski Measurements from the Lyman Alpha Forest and Cosmological Constraints**, arXiv:2607.27410v3. This August 2026 result is used only as current observational context; it does not replace the DR2 Results II chain used for the primary flat-LambdaCDM propagation.

4. DESI public data-license and acknowledgment page: `https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/acknowledgments/`.

5. D. D. Y. Ong, D. Yallup, and W. Handley (2026), **The Bayesian view of DESI DR2 with unimpeded: Evidence and tension in a combined analysis with CMB and supernovae across cosmological models**, arXiv:2603.05472. Used only for the published Bayesian evidence values that define the optional two-model probability layer.

## Recurrence and far-future theory

6. C. Gupta and A. J. Short (2026), **Recurrence Time for Finite Quantum Systems**, arXiv:2604.14995v2. Used for the explicit trace-distance epsilon-recurrence definition and finite-spectrum recurrence-time bounds. CRPS cites the arXiv record only because the journal metadata currently displayed there conflicts with the linked APS article record.

7. G. W. Gibbons and S. W. Hawking (1977), **Cosmological event horizons, thermodynamics, and particle creation**, *Physical Review D* 15, 2738. Used for de Sitter horizon thermodynamics.

8. L. Dyson, M. Kleban, and L. Susskind (2002), **Disturbing Implications of a Cosmological Constant**, JHEP 10 (2002) 011, arXiv:hep-th/0208013. Relevant to finite de Sitter entropy, recurrence, and measure problems.

9. N. Goheer, M. Kleban, and L. Susskind (2003), **The Trouble with de Sitter Space**, JHEP 07 (2003) 056, arXiv:hep-th/0212209.

10. R. R. Caldwell, M. Kamionkowski, and N. N. Weinberg (2003), **Phantom Energy and Cosmic Doomsday**, *Physical Review Letters* 91, 071301, arXiv:astro-ph/0302506. Used for the constant-w phantom finite-lifetime scenario formula.
